# List meetings only from the published feed

Decided on 10 October 2026. The pipeline lists a council's meetings only from its "What's new" feed, `mgRss.aspx`, asked from the council's last complete run. When the feed fails, the council is lost for that run and shows in the report. The two fallbacks were removed from the listing: `GetAllMeetingsByDate` lists meetings by meeting date, not by publish date, so minutes published weeks after a meeting were missed, and the committee scan did the same one committee at a time. Across the six list runs of 9 and 10 October 2026, the by-date call listed meetings once, for East Devon, and the scan never did.

Parked on 10 October 2026: delete the code the fallbacks left behind. In `apps/pipeline`, the ModernGov client still has `getMeetingsByDate`, `getCommittees` and `getMeetingIds`, with their types and tests, and nothing calls them. In `apps/parser-sdk`, the matching address builders and XML readers for those calls are also unused by the pipeline. Remove the pipeline side first, then the SDK side at its next tag, and move the pipeline's pin. Revisit this when the parser SDK is next tagged, or by the end of 2026, whichever comes first.
